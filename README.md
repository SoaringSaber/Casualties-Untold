# The Game
Casualties: Untold is a non-linear story game! Comparable to Telltale games like *Minecraft: Story Mode*, *The Walking Dead (Telltale Adaptation)*, and more, Cas:Untold is fully controlled by you, the player. Your decisions determine your outcome, your reputation, and whether or not you make it out of ▉▉▉▉▉▉ alive, against ▉▉ ▉▉▉▉▉'s wishes.

You control a Sawian named Lemon, a pathetic specimen that is nothing more than a scientist's plaything. You've recently received your one-of-a-kind ▉▉-▉▉▉▉ *brain chip*! Quite the fancy upgrade, isn't it? You've also received word that you're going on a trip?! Is this a dream?? Well, you better prepare for it, it's to die for...

# The Framework
Casualties: Untold features a robust framework that allows for both simple and advanced modifications.

A future release will feature a *Sandbox*, *Custom Character/NPC creator*, and likely more!

Additional information will be posted at a later date.

# Questions? Concerns?

Want to join in development efforts? [Join the discord server](https://discord.gg/UeQuNHhcqt) and ask SoaringSaber! Just... come a little prepared, you have to have *some* skill!

Takedown requests, if any arise, can go to my Discord @soaringsaber. Please, if possible, send a friend request and a DM to ensure I see you. Alternatively, you can create an issue in Github.

Questions and suggestions can go into Github's "Issues" tab as a "feature request".

Feel free to alter the game however you wish, whether it's asset-swapping, recoding (cause Saber's code sucks), or even making your own story, almost anything is possible!
